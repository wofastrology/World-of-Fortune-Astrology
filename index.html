About Me

Since as far back as I can remember, I have always been curious about the mysteries of the Universe. Around the time of my Saturn return, I dove headfirst into the world of astrology and became absolutely hooked. I work to maintain a firm belief that Astrology is a gift from God that we can use to help us navigate and validate our experiences within this soul's lifetime. My practice is rooted in Hellenistic, traditional astrology with a psychological twist to it. I have completed and have become certified in Adam Elenbass' Year 1, 2, and Horary courses, and I am just about done with my Year 3 course as well. I also am in the midst of finishing up Austin Coppock's Year 1 course. If you decide to have a reading with me, I hope I can share some of the same passion that I have for the art of astrology, and our discussion can hopefully provide some guidance or clarity in your soul's journey.

Offerings

In this 45-minute session, the goal is to use your birth chart to provide some insight into whatever questions you have. We can either do a more general Sun, Moon, and Rising meeting, or you could provide a topic(s) that you would like to get into. To enhance the nuance of our discussion, you will need to provide your date, place, and time of birth. For whatever reason, if you do not have your birth time, your date and place of birth will suffice, but there may be some gaps in our discussion.
